TDC Voice privacy policy

Think Define Create LLC · October 11, 2026

TDC Voice stores recordings, audio snapshots, transcripts, bookmarks, app activity and optional monitor screenshots locally on your Mac. Think Define Create does not receive this content. Daily recording starts when you choose Start and stops when you stop it or quit. Screenshots are off by default and capture your displays only after you enable them during daily recording. Microphone and screen/system-audio access are controlled by macOS permissions.

Speech recognition uses downloadable on-device models. After download, transcription can run offline. Model downloads request public files from Hugging Face and its hosting providers, which receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Hugging Face's privacy policy describes automatically recording service-use information, including IP addresses, session information and device information. Its handling of download requests is covered by https://huggingface.co/privacy. TDC Voice does not send recording, screenshot or transcript content in these requests. Optional editing and document generation use Apple's local Apple Intelligence framework on supported systems.

TDC Voice has no developer-operated telemetry, advertising, third-party analytics SDK or tracking identifier. Apple's App Store analytics and crash information may be made available to us under your Apple diagnostics-sharing settings. Apple controls that collection and aggregation. The app keeps a small set of local use counts and dates to space optional review requests; these are not transmitted by TDC Voice. You can disable automatic review requests in Settings when available. A review is never required to use the app.

You choose when to import, export, copy or share your content. Imported originals stay where they were. Local retention settings can remove saved audio/screenshots while preserving transcripts; History also provides deletion controls. Exports, backups, imported originals and copies in other apps are separate files and are not removed by deleting a TDC Voice History entry. Your backup or sync service may copy local files according to its own settings.

Visit website, Send feedback and Write a review open their destinations only when you choose them. Sending feedback opens an email draft addressed to info@thinkdefinecreate.com; no recording, transcript, screenshot or log is attached automatically. Information you voluntarily send to support is used to respond to your request. Our website has separate website analytics and policies; opening it is distinct from recording locally in TDC Voice.

For privacy questions or a support request involving information you shared with us, contact info@thinkdefinecreate.com or visit https://thinkdefinecreate.com. We may update this policy when the app's behavior changes and will publish the current version on the TDC Voice privacy page.

This support website
