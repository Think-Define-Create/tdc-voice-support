TDC Voice support

Contact info@thinkdefinecreate.com. Include your TDC Voice version, macOS version, Mac model and a short description of the problem. Recordings, transcripts and screenshots can contain private information; include them only if you choose to share them. TDC Voice's feedback action attaches nothing automatically.

Getting started

Open TDC Voice from Applications, then click its menu bar icon. In Settings, download a speech model. Models are a separate download and stay on your Mac. Dictate with the microphone, then Copy the text and paste it into your document. The App Store edition uses Copy/Paste. Daily recording saves audio first; transcription is available afterward in History.

Permissions and daily recording

Allow microphone access when you start microphone capture. System audio uses macOS screen/system-audio recording permission. Check Settings → Permissions if capture is blocked; follow macOS's instruction to quit and reopen when needed. Monitor screenshots are optional and must also be enabled in TDC Voice. Choose Start, Pause, Resume, Save snapshot or Stop in the daily recording panel. Keep sufficient disk space available. Check local recording/consent requirements for your use.

Importing a previous installation

Stop recording/transcription and quit the previous app. In the sandbox edition's Settings, choose Import existing library and select the previous OSXVoice folder under ~/Library/Application Support. This imports audio, screenshots, history, bookmarks and original/edited transcripts. Existing entry IDs are skipped, and originals remain in place. A cancelled import keeps completed entries; retry to continue. To reuse an existing speech model, select its version in Settings, then Import model and select its complete folder under ~/Library/Application Support/FluidAudio/Models. Existing downloads must be removed explicitly before replacing them.

Transcription and documents

Downloaded Parakeet models work offline. If a model is incomplete, reset only that version in Settings and download it again. Document generation and optional editing require supported macOS/Apple Intelligence and language settings; recordings and transcripts remain available when these features are unavailable. Review generated text and timestamps before relying on or sharing it. App activity indicates context and does not prove where every captured sound originated.

Storage and exports

History offers search, playback, transcription, bookmarks and export. Select the destination in the native Save panel. Retention settings control saved media; deleting an entry does not remove external exports, original imported folders or backups. See the TDC Voice privacy policy for how information is handled.
